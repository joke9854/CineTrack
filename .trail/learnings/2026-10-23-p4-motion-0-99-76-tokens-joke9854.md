## Decided
- theme/Motion.kt holds everything: durations (Instant/Short/Medium/Long/Extended), M3 easings, snappy/bouncy/gentle springs, enter()/exit() tweens, tabEnter/tabExit (one shared-axis transition for the bottom-bar pages and the Progress sub-tabs), PushEnter/PushExit/PopEnter/PopExit (symmetric pushed pages), PillEnter/PillExit.
- Detail routes keep their own 90 ms fade transitions (the shared poster flight needs them).
- BackupProgressPill now delegates to StatusPill; the pill icon morphs on tone change.
- Season section: the inner AnimatedContent SizeTransforms snap; DetailSectionSurface's animateContentSize is the one size animation. Episode stagger is capped at 6 rows.
- Timeline cards are a flat surface (timelineCard()); glass sits on the bell, the ExpandChevron and the clock.
- Swipe-to-watch: the drag writes a mutableFloatState directly; on release animate() picks up the drag velocity with Motion.snappy().
- Haptics: Confirm for status changes (wrapped once in CineTrackApp), season watched and the Progress check; ToggleOn/Off for the episode circle and the reminder bell; SegmentTick for tab switches and swipe disarm; GestureThresholdActivate on swipe arm.
## Tried and ruled out
- HapticFeedbackType.GestureThresholdDeactivate does not exist in BOM 2026.06.01.
- IntOffset/IntSize.VisibilityThreshold need `import androidx.compose.animation.core.VisibilityThreshold`; they can't be written fully qualified.
## Watch out
- Timeline/history lazy keys contain the position/index, so animateItem there would fade instead of move; not added.
- Read animated values in draw/layout only (WatchedCircle draws its fill and stroke in drawBehind), per P2.
