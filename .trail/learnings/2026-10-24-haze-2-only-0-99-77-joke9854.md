## Decided
- All blur and liquid glass come from Haze 2.0.1 (haze, haze-blur, haze-glass); PrismalAGSL and the JitPack repo are gone. Haze 2 needs Compose 1.12.1 (BOM 2026.09.00) and Kotlin 2.4.20; AGP 9.4.1 built-in Kotlin accepted 2.4.20 with no override. KSP 2.3.4 still works.
- Liquid glass (hazeGlass) is gated at API 33+ and not low-RAM: Haze glass needs RuntimeShader and on API 31-32 drops refraction and blur. Android 12 keeps the flat control material and Haze blur on the bar and sheets.
- Every glass channel is written in GlassTuning's GlassParams.style(): Haze's GlassStyle.regular/clear follow the system light/dark setting, and CineTrack is always dark.
- Inputs are HazeInput.Sources everywhere. Native Backdrop is experimental (37.2 only, flag off) and Haze measured it 20-46% slower for Glass.
- One page HazeState serves the bar and sheets (FloatingGlassHost); AdaptiveBackground's capture is the controls' backdrop (LocalGlassBackdrop); a page with a live blur state reuses it as the glass backdrop.
- Glass values per surface live in ui/components/GlassTuning.kt (defaults tuned by the user on the S23). Debug and preview builds (application id .debug) have a Glass lab overlay (GlassLab.kt, "Glass" tab on the right edge) with live sliders and Copy; values persist in SharedPreferences "glass_lab".

## Tried and ruled out
- Mapping Prismal's settings 1:1: on 40dp buttons a light control tint plus a 6dp refraction band and a centred specular read as a flat grey stroke. The user asked for the nav bar's look; the lab replaced guessing.
- Scroll-driven alpha on hazeBlur: Haze 2 has no alpha lambda (alpha is a Style write). The detail header fades through graphicsLayer alpha instead.

## Watch out
- Haze 2 blur defaults to Balanced (downsampled); Haze 1 was full resolution. Quality is per surface in GlassParams.quality.
- GlassStyle.shape takes only RoundedCornerShape.
- On this 7.3 GB PC installPreview often fails in lintVitalAnalyzePreview from memory pressure; for local installs use -x lintVitalAnalyzePreview -x lintVitalReportPreview and still run lintDebug before committing.
- Phone measurements after 0.99.77 tuning: detail warm jank 3.9-6.3% (0.99.76 ~9.5%), Progress scroll 0.3-1.6%.
